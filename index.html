MISSION BRIEFING

OBJECTIVE — Push north through the jungle, cross the river, breach the fortified camp, and destroy the WARLORD armored patrol vehicle. Reach the extraction flare.

MOVE — WASD / Arrow keys / left stick    AIM — Mouse / right stick

FIRE — Left mouse / right trigger    GRENADE — Space / left trigger or bumper

PAUSE — Esc / P / Start

INTEL — Rifle ammo is unlimited. Grenades are not — crates and fallen foes resupply you. Free captive scouts for bonus points and gear. Chain kills quickly to build your combo multiplier. Barrels and crates explode: use them.

RANK — Score, accuracy, speed, grenade efficiency and rescues decide your final grade: C, B, A or S.
